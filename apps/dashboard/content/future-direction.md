This section separates proposed product directions from capabilities that are
already implemented. A `FUTURE IDEAS` label is not a roadmap commitment, an
approved sprint, or evidence that a provider or technical architecture has
been selected.

Two directions are currently documented: an AI-assisted research control
plane over deterministic framework operations, and a local-first Research
Application for coordinating existing workflows. Neither is part of the
as-built public dashboard capability.
