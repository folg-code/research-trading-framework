Status: **ACTIVE** at the 2026-09-11 review. This is the index for the accepted phase. Increments 16A–16C are complete; 16D Portfolio Dashboard is in progress; 16E–16G remain directional. [Current Status](../CURRENT_STATUS.md) is authoritative for active sprint work. ## Purpose

Bring the independently reached Signal, Strategy, Robustness and Predictive Research runners and dashboard into one research workbench over a neutral Market Analysis catalog. The workflows remain independent; phase approval is not permission to trade or to open every later increment.
